Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

The short version: the OmniMorph program works entirely on your computer and sends me nothing. I only learn about you what is needed to sell you a license, send you your key and help you if you write to me.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Max Mustermann, Musterstraße 1, 12345 Musterstadt, Germany (“I”). Privacy contact: support@example.com.

2. The OmniMorph program on your computer

OmniMorph does not connect to any server, has no telemetry, no analytics and no automatic update check, and does not need an internet connection. Your files are read and converted on your computer only. The program does contact itself on your computer (a local service on the address 127.0.0.1 that its window talks to), and when you click “Buy” it opens the purchase page in your web browser.

The program stores the following on your computer, and none of it is sent to me:

License keyThe key you entered, in %APPDATA%\OmniMorph. A key contains the email address it was issued to.
Trial recordThe time of the first start and how much of the 7-day trial has been used, sealed so that it cannot be edited. It is kept in several places so that the trial can be counted offline: files in %APPDATA%\OmniMorph, %LOCALAPPDATA%\OmniMorph and %PROGRAMDATA%\OmniMorph, and a value under HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\OmniMorph. The seal is tied to your computer using its Windows machine identifier; the identifier itself is not stored in the record and never leaves your computer.
Temporary filesWhile you convert, copies of your files are placed in a temporary folder (omnimorph_jobs_…). They are deleted when the program closes, or after one hour at the latest.
PreferencesYour chosen language, light or dark theme and the last output format you used per file type, stored by the program window on your computer.

Uninstalling the program does not by itself remove the trial record, because removing it would let the trial start again. If you want everything deleted, write to me and I will tell you how, or delete the places listed above after uninstalling. The window of the program is drawn by Microsoft Edge WebView2, a component of Windows that is governed by Microsoft's own privacy terms and settings.

3. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no fonts or scripts from other companies. My web host (GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.)) automatically records technical server logs, such as your IP address, the time and the page requested, to run and secure the site. They are kept for 30 days. Legal basis: my legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

From the order I receive: your name (if given), email address, country, and order details (product, date, amount, order number). I use them to:

  • create your license key and email it to you, and send it again if you lose it (legal basis: performing our contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR);

  • keep accounting and tax records as the law requires (legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c));
  • prevent and deal with misuse of license keys and fraud (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).

I do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not send newsletters unless you ask for them.
