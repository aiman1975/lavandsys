Good to know

  • Scanned PDFs are read with the text recognition (OCR) that is part of Windows. It works offline, but needs the recognition data of the document's language to be installed in Windows (see the FAQ). Handwriting and complicated layouts are not handled well.
  • SVG → PNG/JPG is not offered. Flowcharts and formulas can be exported as SVG, which every browser and Office program can show.
  • Very large tables (over about 150 MB of CSV) are refused with an explanation, to protect your computer's memory. You can raise the limit.
  • Results depend on the source file. Always check important conversions before relying on them.

Questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. OmniMorph does all its work on your computer and does not contact any server – there is no telemetry and no update check. It only opens your web browser when you click a link to an official download page, for example “Get ffmpeg”. The window is drawn by Microsoft Edge WebView2, a component of Windows with its own settings.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download the program. Conversions, the trial and license keys all work offline: keys are checked on your computer.

Can it read scanned PDFs?

Yes, on Windows 10 and 11. Pages that are pictures of text are read with the text recognition (OCR) that is already part of Windows, so it works offline and nothing is uploaded. Windows needs the recognition data for the language of your document. For German, English and Arabic: open Windows Settings → Time & language → Language & region, add the language, open its language options and install “Optical character recognition”. OmniMorph picks the best installed language for each page. If a PDF cannot be read, the message tells you why in plain words.

Some audio conversions say they need ffmpeg. Why?

Converting audio between WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG and AAC uses ffmpeg, a free program made by other people. OmniMorph does not include, download or install it, so you get it from the project's own pages and under its own license. When it is missing, OmniMorph shows how: three short steps, a link to the official ffmpeg download page and a direct download of the Windows build that the ffmpeg project lists (about 180 MB, hosted by a third party). Copy ffmpeg.exe into OmniMorph's tools folder, restart OmniMorph, and the extra conversions appear. Everything else works without it.

What happens when the trial ends?

The program shows a lock screen and stops converting until you enter a license key. You can still look at the list of supported formats and download results you already converted. Your files are never touched.

How do I enter a license key?

In OmniMorph click “Manage License”, paste the key and press Activate. Keys are checked on your computer, without an internet connection.
